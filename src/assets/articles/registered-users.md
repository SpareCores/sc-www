Until now, everything you did on Spare Cores lived in the URL: a carefully
tuned server search, a comparison of a dozen instances, or an Advisor
assessment for your workload. If you wanted to get back to it next week, you
had to bookmark the link in your browser, or dig it out of a Slack thread you
shared with your team.

So we added free user accounts. After a quick registration with your email or
GitHub account, you can save and annotate your research on the site, and
compare or filter without the limits that apply to guests. In short:

- **Bookmarks**: save server and database searches, comparisons, Advisor
  assessments, and individual servers or databases, each with an optional
  note.
- **No limits**: compare any number of servers or databases, and filter by any
  number of countries and regions.
- **One place for everything**: find, sort, and edit all your saved items on
  the new [Bookmarks](/bookmarks) page.

Note that browsing the site stays open to everyone without an account: server
and database listings, benchmark scores, the [Advisor](/advisor), and
comparisons of up to four instances. Comparison links shared with you also keep
working, no matter how many servers they include.

## Sign Up in Seconds

Click "Register" in the top right corner of any page, then either fill in your
name, email address, and password, or continue with GitHub. Email sign-ups get
a verification code in their inbox. Subscribing to our newsletter is optional,
and you can leave it unchecked.

You don't have to register up front either. When you click a feature that
needs an account, such as the bookmark icon next to a server, the registration
window opens right away. After you sign up, the action you clicked is
completed, so the server ends up in your bookmarks without a second click.

Registered users have none of these limits: pick as many regions and countries
as you need on the [server](/servers) and [database](/databases) listings, and
put all of them side by side on the [server](/servers/compare) and
[database](/databases/compare) comparison pages. Combined with
[searching and ordering servers by any benchmark score](/article/search-benchmarks),
this makes it much easier to shortlist the best value servers across all the
locations you care about.

## Bookmarks

You can bookmark the following:

- **Servers and databases**: use the bookmark button on a server or database
  details page, or the bookmark column in the listing tables.
- **Searches**: save the current filters and columns on the [server](/servers)
  or [database](/databases) listing with the bookmark icon next to the page
  title.
- **Comparisons**: save the selected servers or databases from the comparison
  pages.
- **Assessments**: save the recommendations for your workload from the
  [Advisor](/advisor).

Each saved item gets a name and an optional note of up to 2,000 characters, so
you can record why you shortlisted a server or what a comparison was meant to
show.

All your saved items show up on the [Bookmarks](/bookmarks) page, which you can
open from the account menu. Search by name, filter by type (each filter shows
the number of matching items), and sort by name, by the time you bookmarked
them, or by your own order: just drag and drop the cards. You can also rename
items, edit their notes, or delete them from here.
